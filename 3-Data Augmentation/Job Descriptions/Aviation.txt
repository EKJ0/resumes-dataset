DRAFT 1 — Standard / Aircraft Maintenance
================================================
Job Title: Aviation Maintenance Technician

Overview: We're seeking an Aviation Maintenance Technician to inspect, maintain, and
repair aircraft in compliance with FAA and safety regulations.

Key Responsibilities:
- Perform scheduled and unscheduled aircraft inspections and maintenance
- Diagnose and repair mechanical, electrical, and system issues
- Maintain accurate maintenance and compliance records
- Ensure all work meets FAA and safety standards
- Coordinate with flight crews and ground operations

Requirements:
- FAA Airframe & Powerplant (A&P) certification
- 2+ years aircraft maintenance experience
- Strong knowledge of safety and compliance procedures
- Ability to work in a fast-paced, safety-critical environment
