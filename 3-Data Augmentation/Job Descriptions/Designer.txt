JOB DESCRIPTIONS — DESIGNER
Based on a sample of 14 CVs drawn from 303 resumes in the Designer category.

================================================
DRAFT 1 — Standard / Graphic Designer
================================================
Job Title: Graphic Designer

Overview: We're seeking a Graphic Designer to create visually compelling designs
across digital and print materials that align with brand standards.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design marketing materials, logos, and digital assets
- Collaborate with marketing and product teams on creative direction
- Maintain brand consistency across all deliverables
- Manage multiple design projects and deadlines
- Incorporate feedback through iterative design revisions

Requirements:
- Degree in Graphic Design or related field
- Proficiency in Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign)
- Strong portfolio demonstrating range and creativity
- Ability to manage multiple projects under deadline pressure

================================================
DRAFT 2 — Senior / Product-Web Designer
================================================
Job Title: Senior Product Designer

Overview: We're looking for a Senior Designer to lead design strategy across
digital products and marketing experiences.

Key Responsibilities:
- Lead end-to-end design process from concept to final delivery
- Establish and evolve design systems and brand guidelines
- Partner with product and engineering on user experience decisions
- Mentor junior designers and review creative output
- Present design concepts and rationale to stakeholders

Requirements:
- 5+ years design experience, including digital/product design
- Expert proficiency in Adobe Creative Suite and Figma
- Strong portfolio demonstrating strategic design thinking
- Experience building and maintaining design systems
