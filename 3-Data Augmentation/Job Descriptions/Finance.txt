JOB DESCRIPTIONS — FINANCE
Based on a sample of 14 CVs drawn from 142 resumes in the Finance category.

================================================
DRAFT 1 — Standard / Financial Analyst
================================================
Job Title: Financial Analyst

Overview: We're seeking a Financial Analyst to support budgeting, forecasting, and
financial reporting to inform business decisions.

Key Responsibilities:
- Prepare financial models, forecasts, and budget analysis
- Analyze financial performance and identify trends/risks
- Support monthly and quarterly reporting cycles
- Partner with business units on financial planning
- Assist with regulatory and tax reporting requirements

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, or related field
- 2-4 years financial analysis experience
- Strong Excel and financial modeling skills
- Understanding of financial reporting and regulatory requirements

================================================
DRAFT 2 — Senior / Finance Manager
================================================
Job Title: Senior Financial Analyst / Finance Manager

Overview: We're looking for a Senior Financial Analyst to lead financial planning
initiatives and provide strategic insights to leadership.

Key Responsibilities:
- Lead budgeting, forecasting, and long-range financial planning
- Deliver strategic financial insights to senior leadership
- Manage risk analysis and investment evaluation processes
- Ensure compliance with regulatory and tax requirements
- Mentor junior analysts and improve financial processes

Requirements:
- 5+ years finance experience, ideally in investment or corporate finance
- Advanced financial modeling and analytical skills
- Strong understanding of risk, regulatory, and tax considerations
- Proven ability to influence strategic decisions with data

DRAFT 5 — Formal / Corporate
================================================
Job Title: Financial Analyst

Position Summary: The Financial Analyst is responsible for supporting the
organization's financial planning, analysis, and reporting functions to inform
sound business decisions.

Essential Duties and Responsibilities:
- Prepares financial models, forecasts, and budget analyses
- Monitors financial performance against targets and benchmarks
- Supports regulatory, tax, and audit reporting requirements
- Collaborates with business units on financial planning matters
- Presents analysis and recommendations to management

Minimum Qualifications:
- Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, or related field
- Minimum two (2) years of relevant financial analysis experience
- Strong proficiency in Excel and financial modeling
