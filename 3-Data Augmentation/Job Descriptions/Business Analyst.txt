JOB DESCRIPTIONS — BUSINESS ANALYST
Based on a sample of 14 CVs drawn from 133 resumes in the Business Analyst category.

================================================
DRAFT 1 — Standard / Mid-Level
================================================
Job Title: Business Analyst

Overview: We're seeking a Business Analyst to gather requirements, analyze data, and
translate business needs into actionable solutions.

Key Responsibilities:
- Gather and document business and functional requirements
- Analyze data to identify trends, gaps, and opportunities
- Partner with stakeholders and technical teams on solution design
- Support testing, validation, and process improvement initiatives
- Create reports and dashboards to inform business decisions

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Business, Finance, or related field
- 2-4 years business analysis experience
- Proficiency in Excel, SQL, and data visualization tools (e.g., Tableau)
- Strong analytical and stakeholder communication skills

================================================
DRAFT 2 — Senior / Strategic
================================================
Job Title: Senior Business Analyst

Overview: We're looking for a Senior Business Analyst to lead complex requirement-
gathering initiatives and drive data-informed strategic decisions.

Key Responsibilities:
- Lead requirements gathering across cross-functional stakeholders
- Own end-to-end analysis for high-impact business initiatives
- Present findings and recommendations to senior leadership
- Mentor junior analysts and improve analytical processes
- Partner with technical teams to ensure solutions meet business needs

Requirements:
- 5+ years business analysis experience
- Advanced SQL, Excel, and BI tool proficiency (Tableau/Power BI)
- Strong track record influencing strategic decisions with data
- Excellent stakeholder management and presentation skills

================================================
DRAFT 3 — Concise Version
================================================
Job Title: Business Analyst

We need someone who can turn a messy business problem into clear requirements — and
back that up with real data.

You'll:
- Talk to stakeholders and figure out what they actually need
- Dig into the data to find what's really going on
- Turn findings into recommendations people can act on
- Support solutions from requirements through rollout

You bring:
- Business analysis experience
- Comfort with SQL, Excel, and dashboards
- The ability to explain complex things simply

DRAFT 5 — Formal / Corporate
================================================
Job Title: Business Analyst

Position Summary: The Business Analyst is responsible for eliciting, analyzing, and
documenting business requirements to support data-driven decision-making and
process improvement.

Essential Duties and Responsibilities:
- Conducts requirements-gathering sessions with business stakeholders
- Performs quantitative and qualitative analysis of business data
- Develops reports, dashboards, and documentation to support decisions
- Collaborates with IT and operations teams on solution implementation
- Monitors and evaluates outcomes of implemented recommendations

Minimum Qualifications:
- Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Finance, or related field
- Minimum two (2) years of business analysis experience
- Proficiency in SQL, Excel, and business intelligence tools
