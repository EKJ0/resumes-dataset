================================================
DRAFT 1 — Standard / Mid-Level
================================================
Job Title: Civil Engineer

Overview: We're seeking a Civil Engineer to design, plan, and oversee construction
and infrastructure projects from concept through completion.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design civil infrastructure projects (site, structural, water) using AutoCAD
- Prepare plans, specifications, and cost estimates
- Coordinate with contractors, clients, and regulatory agencies
- Monitor construction sites for quality and code compliance
- Manage project timelines and budgets

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Civil Engineering
- 3-5 years relevant engineering experience; PE license a plus
- Proficiency in AutoCAD and civil design software
- Strong understanding of construction methods and codes

DRAFT 2 — Senior / Project Engineer
================================================
Job Title: Senior Civil Engineer / Project Engineer

Overview: We're looking for a Senior Civil Engineer to lead infrastructure projects,
manage engineering teams, and ensure delivery within budget and schedule.

Key Responsibilities:
- Lead design and delivery of commercial/infrastructure projects
- Manage project budgets, schedules, and multi-million dollar scopes
- Supervise junior engineers and coordinate with multidisciplinary teams
- Ensure compliance with structural, environmental, and safety standards
- Serve as primary technical point of contact for clients

Requirements:
- PE license required
- 7+ years civil engineering experience, including project leadership
- Advanced AutoCAD/civil design software proficiency
- Proven record delivering large-scale projects on budget

Job Title: Civil Engineer

Position Summary: The Civil Engineer is responsible for the design, planning, and
oversight of infrastructure and construction projects, ensuring compliance with

- Prepares engineering designs, drawings, and specifications
- Develops project cost estimates and monitors budget performance
