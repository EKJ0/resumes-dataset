JOB DESCRIPTIONS — CONSTRUCTION
Based on a sample of 14 CVs drawn from 55 resumes in the Construction category.

================================================
DRAFT 1 — Standard / Construction Manager
================================================
Job Title: Construction Manager

Overview: We're seeking a Construction Manager to oversee project sites, safety
compliance, budgets, and subcontractor coordination.

Key Responsibilities:
- Manage day-to-day construction site activities and scheduling
- Oversee project budgets and cost control
- Ensure compliance with safety regulations and quality standards
- Coordinate subcontractors, suppliers, and labor
- Track project progress against milestones and completion targets

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Construction Management or related field
- 4+ years construction management experience
- Strong knowledge of safety regulations and building codes
- Proven ability to manage multi-million dollar project budgets

================================================
DRAFT 2 — Site Superintendent
================================================
Job Title: Construction Superintendent

Overview: We're looking for a hands-on Superintendent to run daily site operations
and ensure work is completed safely, on time, and to spec.

Key Responsibilities:
- Supervise daily on-site construction activities and crews
- Enforce safety standards and conduct site inspections
- Coordinate scheduling across trades and subcontractors
- Resolve on-site issues quickly to avoid delays
- Report progress and issues to project management

Requirements:
- 5+ years construction supervision experience
- OSHA certification preferred
- Strong scheduling and problem-solving skills
- Comfortable managing multiple trades on-site

DRAFT 5 — Formal / Corporate
================================================
Job Title: Construction Manager

Position Summary: The Construction Manager is responsible for overseeing project
sites to ensure work is completed safely, on schedule, within budget, and to
required quality standards.

Essential Duties and Responsibilities:
- Manages construction schedules, budgets, and resource allocation
- Ensures compliance with safety regulations and building codes
- Coordinates subcontractors, suppliers, and site personnel
- Conducts regular site inspections and quality reviews
- Reports project status and issues to senior management

Minimum Qualifications:
- Bachelor's degree in Construction Management, Engineering, or related field
- Minimum four (4) years of construction management experience
- OSHA certification preferred
