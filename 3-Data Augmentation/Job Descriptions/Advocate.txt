JOB DESCRIPTIONS — ADVOCATE
Based on a sample of 14 CVs drawn from 341 resumes in the Advocate category.
(Note: this dataset's "Advocate" category spans legal advocates and client/customer
advocacy roles — drafts below cover both angles.)

================================================
DRAFT 1 — Legal Advocate, Standard
================================================
Job Title: Advocate

Overview: We are seeking an Advocate to represent client interests, provide legal
guidance, and manage case documentation with accuracy and diligence.

Key Responsibilities:
- Represent and advise clients on legal matters and case strategy
- Draft, review, and maintain legal records and case files
- Liaise with courts, opposing counsel, and relevant authorities
- Ensure compliance with legal procedures and regulations
- Maintain clear, timely communication with clients

Requirements:
- Law degree and valid license to practice
- Strong research, negotiation, and communication skills
- Experience managing case files and legal documentation
- High ethical standards and attention to detail

================================================
DRAFT 2 — Client/Customer Advocate
================================================
Job Title: Customer Advocate

Overview: We're looking for a Customer Advocate to serve as the primary point of
contact for client needs, resolving issues and building lasting relationships.

Key Responsibilities:
- Respond to customer inquiries and resolve issues promptly
- Maintain accurate records of customer interactions and case history
- Coordinate across teams to address client needs and improve service quality
- Identify recurring issues and recommend process improvements
- Support clients through onboarding, service changes, and escalations

Requirements:
- 2+ years in customer service, support, or advocacy roles
- Strong communication and conflict-resolution skills
- Comfortable managing multiple accounts/cases simultaneously
- Proficiency with CRM or case management systems

DRAFT 3 — Concise Version
================================================
Job Title: Advocate

We're looking for someone who fights for the people they represent — whether that's
clients, patients, or customers — and keeps meticulous records while doing it.

You'll:
- Be the trusted point of contact for those you represent
- Track and resolve cases/issues from intake to close
- Keep documentation airtight and up to date
- Work cross-functionally to remove obstacles for your clients

You bring:
- Strong communication and relationship-building skills
- Experience in an advocacy, legal, or client-facing role
- Comfort navigating policy, procedure, and ambiguity

DRAFT 5 — Formal / Corporate
================================================
Job Title: Advocate

Position Summary: The Advocate serves as a representative for client or constituent
interests, ensuring their needs are addressed in accordance with organizational
policy and, where applicable, legal standards.

Essential Duties and Responsibilities:
- Represents and advises clients/constituents on relevant matters
- Maintains comprehensive and accurate case documentation
- Coordinates with internal departments and external parties as needed
- Ensures all interactions comply with applicable policies and regulations
- Identifies opportunities to improve service delivery and outcomes

Minimum Qualifications:
- Relevant degree (law, social work, or related field depending on specialization)
- Prior experience in an advocacy, legal, or client relations capacity
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills
