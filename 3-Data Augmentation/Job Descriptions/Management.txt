JOB DESCRIPTIONS — MANAGEMENT
Based on a sample of 14 CVs drawn from 300 resumes in the Management category.
(This dataset's "Management" category spans several industries — drafts below are
written as a general operations/business management role.)

================================================
DRAFT 1 — Standard / Operations Manager
================================================
Job Title: Manager

Overview: We're seeking a Manager to oversee daily operations, staff performance,
and service delivery within their area of responsibility.

Key Responsibilities:
- Manage daily operations and ensure service/quality standards are met
- Supervise, train, and develop staff
- Monitor performance metrics and drive continuous improvement
- Manage budgets, resources, and vendor relationships
- Ensure compliance with company policies and procedures

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Business or related field
- 3+ years management or supervisory experience
- Strong leadership and organizational skills
- Experience managing budgets and performance metrics

================================================
DRAFT 2 — Senior / Regional Manager
================================================
Job Title: Senior Manager

Overview: We're looking for a Senior Manager to lead multi-site or departmental
operations and drive strategic performance improvement.

Key Responsibilities:
- Lead operations across multiple sites or departments
- Develop and execute strategic plans to meet business goals
- Manage P&L, budgets, and resource allocation
- Build and develop high-performing management teams
- Report performance and strategic recommendations to leadership

Requirements:
- 6+ years management experience, including multi-site leadership
- Proven track record improving operational and financial performance
- Strong strategic planning and leadership skills
- Excellent stakeholder communication skills
