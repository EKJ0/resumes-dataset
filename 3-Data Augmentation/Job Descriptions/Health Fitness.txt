================================================
DRAFT 1 — Standard / Personal Trainer
================================================
Job Title: Personal Trainer

Overview: We're seeking a Personal Trainer to design and deliver individualized
fitness programs that help clients reach their health goals.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design personalized exercise and nutrition programs for clients
- Conduct one-on-one and group fitness sessions
- Track client progress and adjust programs accordingly
- Ensure safe and proper use of gym equipment
- Motivate and educate clients on healthy lifestyle habits

Requirements:
- Certified Personal Trainer credential (e.g., ACE, NASM)
- CPR/First Aid certification
- 1+ years personal training or coaching experience
- Strong interpersonal and motivational skills

DRAFT 2 — Fitness Manager / Coach

Job Title: Fitness Program Manager

Overview: We're looking for a Fitness Program Manager to lead class programming,
trainer staff, and member engagement at our facility.

Key Responsibilities:
- Develop and manage group fitness class schedules and programming
- Recruit, train, and supervise fitness instructors/trainers
- Drive member engagement and retention initiatives
- Ensure facility safety and equipment maintenance standards
- Track program performance and member satisfaction

Requirements:
- 3+ years fitness industry management experience
- Relevant certification (ACE, NASM, or equivalent)
- Strong leadership and program development skills
- Bachelor's degree in Exercise Science preferred
