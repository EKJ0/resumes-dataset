DRAFT 1 — Standard / PR Specialist
================================================
Job Title: Public Relations Specialist

Overview: We're seeking a PR Specialist to manage media relations, press
communications, and brand reputation initiatives.

Key Responsibilities:
- Draft and distribute press releases and media materials
- Build and maintain relationships with media contacts and outlets
- Monitor media coverage and manage brand reputation
- Support event planning and PR campaign execution
- Coordinate with marketing on integrated communications strategy

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Communications, PR, or related field
- 2+ years public relations or communications experience
- Strong writing and media relationship skills
- Experience with press release distribution and media monitoring
