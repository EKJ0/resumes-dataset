JOB DESCRIPTIONS — DEVOPS ENGINEER
Based on a sample of 14 CVs drawn from 276 resumes in the DevOps Engineer category.

================================================
DRAFT 1 — Standard / Mid-Level
================================================
Job Title: DevOps Engineer

Overview: We're seeking a DevOps Engineer to build and maintain CI/CD pipelines,
cloud infrastructure, and automation across our engineering environment.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design, build, and maintain CI/CD pipelines (Jenkins or similar)
- Manage cloud infrastructure on AWS/Azure using infrastructure-as-code
- Deploy and manage containerized applications (Docker, Kubernetes)
- Automate operational tasks and improve deployment reliability
- Monitor system performance and troubleshoot production issues

Requirements:
- 3+ years DevOps or infrastructure engineering experience
- Proficiency with AWS/Azure, Docker, and Kubernetes
- Experience with CI/CD tools (Jenkins) and IaC (Terraform, Ansible)
- Strong scripting skills (Python or similar)

DRAFT 2 — Senior / Infrastructure Lead

Job Title: Senior DevOps Engineer

Overview: We're looking for a Senior DevOps Engineer to own infrastructure

- Lead CI/CD strategy and drive continuous delivery best practices

- Mentor engineers on DevOps and cloud best practices

Requirements:
- 6+ years DevOps/SRE experience
- Deep expertise in AWS/Azure, Kubernetes, Docker, and Terraform
- Strong track record improving deployment velocity and reliability
- Experience with security and compliance in cloud environments

DRAFT 5 — Formal / Corporate
================================================
Job Title: DevOps Engineer

Position Summary: The DevOps Engineer is responsible for building and maintaining
the infrastructure, automation, and deployment pipelines that support reliable
software delivery.

Essential Duties and Responsibilities:
- Designs and maintains CI/CD pipelines for application deployment
- Manages cloud infrastructure using infrastructure-as-code practices
- Deploys and monitors containerized applications and services
- Implements automation to reduce manual operational work
- Collaborates with engineering teams to improve system reliability

Minimum Qualifications:
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field
- Minimum three (3) years of DevOps or infrastructure engineering experience
- Proficiency with cloud platforms, containerization, and CI/CD tools
