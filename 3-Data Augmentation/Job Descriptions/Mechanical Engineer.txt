JOB DESCRIPTIONS — MECHANICAL ENGINEER
Based on a sample of 14 CVs drawn from 185 resumes in the Mechanical Engineer category.

================================================
DRAFT 1 — Standard / Mid-Level
================================================
Job Title: Mechanical Engineer

Overview: We're seeking a Mechanical Engineer to design, test, and improve
mechanical systems and manufacturing processes.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design mechanical components and systems using CAD tools
- Perform testing, analysis, and troubleshooting of equipment
- Support manufacturing processes and production efficiency initiatives
- Prepare technical drawings and specifications
- Collaborate with cross-functional engineering teams

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering
- 2-5 years relevant engineering experience
- Proficiency in CAD software and thermodynamics principles
- Strong analytical and troubleshooting skills

================================================
DRAFT 2 — Senior / HVAC-Manufacturing Focus
================================================
Job Title: Senior Mechanical Engineer

Overview: We're looking for a Senior Mechanical Engineer to lead design projects
and drive efficiency and reliability improvements across systems.

Key Responsibilities:
- Lead design of complex mechanical systems (HVAC, manufacturing, or product)
- Drive efficiency and reliability improvement initiatives
- Oversee testing, validation, and production support
- Mentor junior engineers and review technical designs
- Manage project scope, budget, and timelines

Requirements:
- 6+ years mechanical engineering experience
- Advanced CAD and analysis tool proficiency
- Track record delivering measurable efficiency/reliability gains
- Strong leadership and project management skills
