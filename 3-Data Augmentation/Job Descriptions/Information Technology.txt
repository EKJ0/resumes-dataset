JOB DESCRIPTIONS — INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY
Based on a sample of 14 CVs drawn from 270 resumes in the Information Technology category.

================================================
DRAFT 1 — Standard / IT Support Specialist
================================================
Job Title: IT Support Specialist

Overview: We're seeking an IT Support Specialist to provide technical support for
hardware, software, and network issues across the organization.

Key Responsibilities:
- Provide first- and second-level technical support to end users
- Troubleshoot hardware, software, and network issues
- Maintain IT documentation and equipment inventory
- Support system security and access management
- Escalate complex issues to appropriate technical teams

Requirements:
- Associate's or Bachelor's degree in IT or related field
- 2+ years IT support experience
- Working knowledge of Windows systems and networking basics
- Strong troubleshooting and customer service skills

================================================
DRAFT 2 — Senior / IT Systems Administrator
================================================
Job Title: IT Systems Administrator

Overview: We're looking for a Systems Administrator to manage enterprise IT
infrastructure, security, and technical operations.

Key Responsibilities:
- Administer and maintain enterprise servers, networks, and systems
- Implement and monitor IT security policies and procedures
- Lead technical projects and system implementations
- Manage vendor relationships and technology procurement
- Provide escalated technical support and troubleshooting

Requirements:
- 5+ years IT infrastructure/systems administration experience
- Strong knowledge of networking, security, and enterprise systems
- Experience leading IT projects and implementations
- Relevant certifications (e.g., CompTIA, Microsoft) a plus
