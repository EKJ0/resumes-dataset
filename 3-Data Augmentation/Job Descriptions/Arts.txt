JOB DESCRIPTIONS — ARTS
Based on a sample of 14 CVs drawn from 323 resumes in the Arts category.
(This dataset's "Arts" category is dominated by teaching/education-adjacent arts
roles — drafts below cover art education and general arts/creative roles.)

================================================
DRAFT 1 — Art Teacher, Standard
================================================
Job Title: Art Teacher

Overview: We're seeking an Art Teacher to design and deliver engaging visual arts
curriculum for students across grade levels.

Key Responsibilities:
- Plan and deliver lesson plans aligned with curriculum standards
- Foster student creativity through diverse artistic mediums
- Assess student progress and provide constructive feedback
- Manage classroom materials, supplies, and safety
- Organize student art exhibitions and showcases

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Art Education or related field
- Teaching certification (where required)
- Strong classroom management and communication skills
- Experience across multiple art mediums

================================================
DRAFT 2 — Visual/Fine Artist
================================================
Job Title: Visual Artist

Overview: We're looking for a Visual Artist to create original work and contribute to
creative projects across a range of media.

Key Responsibilities:
- Develop original artwork/concepts aligned with project briefs
- Collaborate with creative and production teams
- Manage multiple projects and deadlines simultaneously
- Present concepts and revisions to stakeholders
- Maintain a portfolio reflecting current creative direction

Requirements:
- Degree in Fine Arts or equivalent portfolio experience
- Proficiency in relevant creative tools/mediums
- Strong conceptual and technical execution skills

================================================
DRAFT 3 — Concise Version
================================================
Job Title: Arts Educator / Creative Professional

We're looking for someone who can bring creativity into a classroom or studio and
make it stick.

You'll:
- Plan and lead engaging arts-focused lessons or projects
- Give students/collaborators real, useful feedback
- Keep materials, space, and schedules organized
- Showcase finished work with pride

You bring:
- A background in art, education, or both
- Genuine enthusiasm for creative development in others
- Strong organizational habits

================================================
DRAFT 4 — Skills/Tools-Focused
================================================
Job Title: Arts Program Coordinator

Core Skills Required: curriculum development, lesson planning, classroom
management, multiple art mediums, student assessment, program/event coordination.

Responsibilities:
- Design and deliver arts curriculum or programming
- Track and report on student/participant progress
- Coordinate exhibitions, showcases, or program events
- Manage supplies, budget, and program logistics
- Build relationships with students, parents, or stakeholders

Qualifications:
- Relevant degree in arts, education, or related field
- Experience teaching or coordinating creative programs
- Strong communication and planning skills

================================================
DRAFT 5 — Formal / Corporate
================================================
Job Title: Art Teacher

Position Summary: The Art Teacher is responsible for developing and delivering
visual arts instruction that fosters student creativity, technical skill, and
appreciation for the arts.

Essential Duties and Responsibilities:
- Develops lesson plans aligned with curriculum standards and learning objectives
- Instructs students in a variety of artistic techniques and mediums
- Evaluates student work and provides constructive, developmental feedback
- Maintains classroom safety, materials, and equipment
- Organizes student exhibitions and community engagement events

Minimum Qualifications:
- Bachelor's degree in Art Education, Fine Arts, or related field
- Valid teaching certification as applicable
- Prior classroom or instructional experience preferred
