JOB DESCRIPTIONS — DIGITAL MEDIA
Based on a sample of 14 CVs drawn from 282 resumes in the Digital Media category.

================================================
DRAFT 1 — Standard / Digital Marketing Specialist
================================================
Job Title: Digital Media Specialist

Overview: We're seeking a Digital Media Specialist to plan and execute digital
marketing campaigns across social, search, and email channels.

Key Responsibilities:
- Plan and execute digital campaigns across social media, search, and email
- Manage content calendars and campaign creative
- Analyze campaign performance using Google Analytics and reporting tools
- Manage paid social/search budgets to meet performance goals
- Collaborate with creative teams on brand-aligned content

Requirements:
- Degree in Marketing, Communications, or related field
- 2+ years digital marketing experience
- Proficiency with Google Analytics and social media platforms
- Strong writing and campaign analysis skills

================================================
DRAFT 2 — Senior / Digital Media Manager
================================================
Job Title: Digital Media Manager

Overview: We're looking for a Digital Media Manager to own digital strategy, drive
audience growth, and lead campaign execution across channels.

Key Responsibilities:
- Develop and execute digital media strategy across all channels
- Manage advertising budgets and optimize for ROI
- Lead a team executing content, social, and paid campaigns
- Analyze performance data to inform ongoing strategy
- Present campaign results and recommendations to leadership

Requirements:
- 5+ years digital marketing/media experience
- Proven track record managing multi-channel campaigns and budgets
- Strong analytics skills (Google Analytics, campaign reporting)
- Leadership and cross-functional collaboration experience
