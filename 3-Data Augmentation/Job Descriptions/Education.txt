DRAFT 1 — Standard / Classroom Teacher
================================================
Job Title: Teacher

Overview: We're seeking a dedicated Teacher to plan and deliver instruction that
meets the individual needs of every student.

Key Responsibilities:
- Develop and deliver lesson plans aligned with curriculum standards
- Assess student progress and adapt instruction to individual needs
- Maintain a positive, safe, and structured classroom environment
- Collaborate with parents, staff, and administrators on student support
- Manage classroom administrative tasks and documentation

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Education or subject area
- Valid teaching certification
- Strong classroom management and communication skills
- Experience differentiating instruction for diverse learners

DRAFT 2 — Special Education Teacher
================================================
Job Title: Special Education Teacher

Overview: We're looking for a Special Education Teacher to design and deliver
individualized instruction and support for students with special needs.

Key Responsibilities:
- Develop and implement Individualized Education Programs (IEPs)
- Provide specialized instruction tailored to each student's needs
- Collaborate with parents, therapists, and support staff
- Monitor and document student progress toward IEP goals
- Ensure a safe, supportive, and inclusive classroom environment

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Special Education
- Special education teaching certification
- Experience developing and implementing IEPs
- Patience, empathy, and strong communication skills
