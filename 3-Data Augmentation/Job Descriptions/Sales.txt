JOB DESCRIPTIONS — SALES
Based on a sample of 14 CVs drawn from 164 resumes in the Sales category.

================================================
DRAFT 1 — Standard / Sales Representative
================================================
Job Title: Sales Representative

Overview: We're seeking a Sales Representative to build customer relationships,
drive revenue growth, and exceed sales targets.

Key Responsibilities:
- Identify and pursue new sales opportunities within assigned territory
- Build and maintain strong customer relationships
- Meet or exceed quarterly sales quotas and revenue targets
- Develop and execute account-specific sales strategies
- Track pipeline activity and report on sales performance

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Business or related field preferred
- 2+ years sales experience with a track record of meeting quotas
- Strong relationship-building and negotiation skills
- Self-motivated with excellent communication skills

================================================
DRAFT 2 — Senior / Sales Manager
================================================
Job Title: Sales Manager

Overview: We're looking for a Sales Manager to lead a sales team, develop growth
strategy, and drive revenue across the territory or market.

Key Responsibilities:
- Lead and coach a team of sales representatives
- Develop and execute strategic sales plans to drive growth
- Set and track sales targets and quota attainment
- Analyze market trends to identify new business opportunities
- Build key client and partner relationships

Requirements:
- 5+ years sales experience, including team leadership
- Proven track record exceeding revenue targets
- Strong strategic planning and coaching skills
- Excellent relationship-building and negotiation skills
