DRAFT 1 — Standard / Business Consultant
================================================
Job Title: Business Consultant

Overview: We're seeking a Business Consultant to help clients solve operational and
strategic challenges through analysis, planning, and hands-on delivery.

Key Responsibilities:
- Assess client business processes and identify improvement opportunities
- Develop and present recommendations and strategic plans
- Manage client relationships and project delivery
- Support implementation of process and performance improvements
- Analyze data to inform strategy and measure impact

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Business, Finance, or related field
- 3+ years consulting or relevant business experience
- Strong analytical, communication, and project management skills
- Comfortable managing multiple client engagements
