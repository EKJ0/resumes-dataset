Job Title: Electrical Engineer

Overview: We're seeking an Electrical Engineer to design, test, and maintain
electrical systems and equipment across projects.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design electrical systems and prepare technical specifications
- Perform testing, troubleshooting, and inspection of equipment
- Support installation and commissioning of electrical systems
- Ensure designs meet safety and regulatory standards
- Collaborate with cross-functional engineering teams

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering
- 2-5 years relevant engineering experience
- Proficiency in CAD and electrical design tools
- Strong troubleshooting and analytical skills
