JOB DESCRIPTIONS — PMO (PROJECT MANAGEMENT OFFICE)
Based on a sample of 14 CVs drawn from 96 resumes in the PMO category.

================================================
DRAFT 1 — Standard / PMO Analyst-Coordinator
================================================
Job Title: PMO Analyst

Overview: We're seeking a PMO Analyst to support project governance, reporting,
and portfolio coordination across the organization.

Key Responsibilities:
- Track project status, risks, and issues across the portfolio
- Prepare executive reporting and dashboards for project performance
- Support project budgeting, scheduling, and change management
- Maintain PMO documentation and process standards
- Coordinate with project managers on governance compliance

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Business, IT, or related field
- 2+ years project coordination or PMO experience
- Familiarity with Agile and traditional project methodologies
- Strong organizational and reporting skills

================================================
DRAFT 2 — Senior / PMO Manager
================================================
Job Title: PMO Manager

Overview: We're looking for a PMO Manager to lead project governance standards,
portfolio reporting, and cross-functional program delivery.

Key Responsibilities:
- Establish and enforce PMO governance standards and processes
- Oversee portfolio-level reporting, risk, and financial tracking
- Lead cross-functional program delivery and stakeholder alignment
- Manage relationships with senior/executive stakeholders
- Drive continuous improvement of PMO tools and processes

Requirements:
- 6+ years project/program management experience, including PMO leadership
- Strong knowledge of Agile and portfolio management practices
- Proven experience presenting to executive stakeholders
- PMP or equivalent certification preferred
