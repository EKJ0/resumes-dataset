DRAFT 1 — Standard / Mid-Level
================================================
Job Title: SQL Developer

Overview: We're seeking a SQL Developer to design, build, and optimize database
solutions supporting business applications and reporting.

Key Responsibilities:
- Write and optimize complex SQL/PL-SQL queries and stored procedures
- Design database schemas and support ETL/SSIS processes
- Troubleshoot performance issues and tune database queries
- Maintain data integrity and support reporting requirements
- Collaborate with development teams on database design

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field
- 2-4 years SQL development experience
- Proficiency in Oracle/SQL Server, PL/SQL, and SSIS
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
