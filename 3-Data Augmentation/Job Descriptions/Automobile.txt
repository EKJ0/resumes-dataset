DRAFT 1 — Standard / Automotive Technician
================================================
Job Title: Automotive Technician

Overview: We're seeking an Automotive Technician to diagnose, repair, and maintain
vehicles to the highest standards of quality and safety.

Key Responsibilities:
- Diagnose mechanical, electrical, and system issues in vehicles
- Perform routine maintenance including brakes, engine, and suspension work
- Complete repairs accurately and within expected timeframes
- Maintain detailed service and repair records
- Ensure customer satisfaction through quality workmanship

Requirements:
- ASE certification preferred
- 2+ years automotive repair experience
- Strong diagnostic and problem-solving skills
- Familiarity with diagnostic tools and repair equipment

DRAFT 5 — Formal / Corporate
================================================
Job Title: Automotive Technician

Position Summary: The Automotive Technician is responsible for the accurate
diagnosis, repair, and maintenance of customer vehicles in accordance with
manufacturer and safety standards.

Essential Duties and Responsibilities:
- Performs diagnostic evaluations to identify mechanical and electrical issues
- Executes repairs and routine maintenance per established procedures
- Maintains accurate and complete service records
- Ensures compliance with shop safety standards and procedures
- Communicates repair recommendations to service advisors and customers

Minimum Qualifications:
- Completion of automotive technology training program
- ASE certification preferred; required for senior roles
- Minimum two (2) years of relevant repair experience
