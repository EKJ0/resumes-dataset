JOB DESCRIPTIONS — ACCOUNTANT
Based on a sample of 14 CVs drawn from 351 resumes in the Accountant category.
Five alternate drafts below — use as-is or blend.

================================================
DRAFT 1 — Standard / Mid-Level
================================================
Job Title: Accountant

Overview: We are seeking a detail-oriented Accountant to manage day-to-day financial
operations, ensure accurate reporting, and support month-end and year-end close.

Key Responsibilities:
- Prepare and review financial statements, reports, and reconciliations
- Manage accounts payable/receivable, cash transactions, and payroll support
- Perform month-end and year-end closing procedures in accordance with GAAP
- Maintain accurate financial records and resolve discrepancies
- Support internal and external audits

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or related field
- 2-5 years of accounting experience
- Strong knowledge of GAAP and financial reporting standards
- Proficiency with accounting software and Excel
- High attention to detail and accuracy

================================================
DRAFT 2 — Senior / CPA-Track
================================================
Job Title: Senior Accountant

Overview: We're looking for a CPA-track Senior Accountant to own financial close,
tax compliance, and reporting accuracy for a growing finance team.

Key Responsibilities:
- Lead monthly, quarterly, and annual close processes
- Prepare tax filings and ensure regulatory compliance
- Review journal entries, account reconciliations, and financial statements
- Partner with auditors and provide audit documentation
- Mentor junior accounting staff and improve close procedures

Requirements:
- CPA or CPA candidate strongly preferred
- 5+ years progressive accounting experience
- Deep knowledge of GAAP, tax regulations, and internal controls
- Advanced Excel and ERP system experience
- Proven track record improving accuracy and closing timelines

================================================
DRAFT 3 — Concise / Startup Style
================================================
Job Title: Staff Accountant

We need someone who's fast, accurate, and comfortable owning the books end to end.

You'll:
- Keep our books current — AP, AR, reconciliations, payroll support
- Close the month on time, every time
- Flag discrepancies before they become problems
- Build simple reporting our leadership actually reads

You bring:
- 2+ years of hands-on accounting experience
- Comfort with ambiguity and evolving processes
- Solid Excel/spreadsheet skills
- A bias toward accuracy over speed (but you're quick too)

DRAFT 5 — Formal / Corporate
================================================
Job Title: Accountant

Position Summary: The Accountant is responsible for maintaining the integrity of the
organization's financial records, ensuring compliance with applicable accounting
standards, and supporting the finance department's reporting obligations.

Essential Duties and Responsibilities:
- Prepares and analyzes financial statements and management reports
- Administers accounts payable, accounts receivable, and payroll functions
- Executes month-end and year-end closing procedures per GAAP
- Ensures compliance with internal policies, tax regulations, and audit requirements
- Reconciles general ledger accounts and investigates variances

Minimum Qualifications:
- Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related discipline
- Minimum three (3) years of relevant professional experience
- Demonstrated proficiency in accounting software and Microsoft Excel
- Strong written and verbal communication skills
