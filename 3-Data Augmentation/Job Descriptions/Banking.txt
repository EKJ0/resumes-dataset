JOB DESCRIPTIONS — BANKING
Based on a sample of 14 CVs drawn from 262 resumes in the Banking category.

================================================
DRAFT 1 — Standard / Bank Teller-Officer
================================================
Job Title: Banking Officer

Overview: We're seeking a Banking Officer to manage customer transactions, cash
operations, and account services with accuracy and professionalism.

Key Responsibilities:
- Process customer transactions including deposits, withdrawals, and transfers
- Balance cash drawers and reconcile daily transactions
- Open and manage customer accounts and banking products
- Ensure compliance with banking policies and regulations
- Build lasting relationships with branch customers

Requirements:
- 2+ years banking or financial services experience
- Strong cash handling and accuracy skills
- Knowledge of banking compliance and procedures
- Excellent customer service skills

DRAFT 5 — Formal / Corporate
================================================
Job Title: Banking Officer

Position Summary: The Banking Officer is responsible for delivering accurate,
compliant transaction processing and account services while supporting the branch's
sales and customer service objectives.

Essential Duties and Responsibilities:
- Processes customer transactions in accordance with policy and regulation
- Maintains and reconciles cash drawer balances daily
- Opens, maintains, and services customer accounts
- Identifies opportunities to promote relevant banking products
- Ensures full compliance with applicable banking regulations

Minimum Qualifications:
- High school diploma required; Bachelor's degree in Finance/Business preferred
- Minimum two (2) years of banking or financial services experience
- Strong customer service and cash handling skills
