DRAFT 1 — Standard / Food & Beverage Manager
================================================
Job Title: Food & Beverage Manager

Overview: We're seeking a Food & Beverage Manager to oversee daily restaurant/
hospitality operations, staff, and guest experience.

Key Responsibilities:
- Manage daily food and beverage service operations
- Supervise, train, and schedule staff
- Ensure quality, consistency, and food safety standards
- Manage inventory, ordering, and cost control
- Monitor guest satisfaction and resolve service issues

Requirements:
- 3+ years food & beverage or hospitality management experience
- Strong knowledge of food safety and service standards
- Proven staff supervision and training experience
- Strong organizational and cost-control skills
