Based on a sample of 14 CVs drawn from 244 resumes in the ETL Developer category.

================================================
DRAFT 1 — Standard / Mid-Level
================================================
Job Title: ETL Developer

Overview: We're seeking an ETL Developer to design and build data pipelines that
extract, transform, and load data across systems.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design and develop ETL mappings and workflows (Informatica/SSIS)
- Extract, transform, and load data from multiple source systems
- Optimize ETL jobs for performance and reliability
- Test and validate data quality across pipelines
- Support data warehouse and reporting initiatives

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field
- 3+ years ETL development experience (Informatica, SSIS, or similar)
- Strong SQL and Oracle/SQL Server experience
- Familiarity with UNIX scripting a plus

maintaining data integration pipelines that support enterprise reporting and
