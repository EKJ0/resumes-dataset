JOB DESCRIPTIONS — APPAREL
Based on a sample of 14 CVs drawn from 120 resumes in the Apparel category.

================================================
DRAFT 1 — Standard / Merchandising
================================================
Job Title: Apparel Merchandiser

Overview: We're seeking an Apparel Merchandiser to manage product assortment,
inventory, and in-store presentation to drive sales and brand consistency.

Key Responsibilities:
- Plan and manage seasonal apparel assortments and inventory levels
- Coordinate merchandising displays and floor sets
- Analyze sales trends to inform buying and restocking decisions
- Partner with design and production teams on line development
- Ensure brand and quality standards across merchandise

Requirements:
- Experience in apparel, fashion, or retail merchandising
- Strong eye for trends, color, and product presentation
- Familiarity with inventory and sales reporting tools
- Bachelor's degree in Fashion Merchandising or related field preferred

================================================
DRAFT 2 — Apparel Designer
================================================
Job Title: Apparel Designer

Overview: We're looking for a creative Apparel Designer to develop garment concepts
from sketch through production-ready specs.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design seasonal apparel lines aligned with brand and market trends
- Create tech packs, fit specifications, and construction details
- Source fabrics, trims, and materials
- Collaborate with production teams to ensure design integrity
- Present concept boards and seasonal collections to stakeholders

Requirements:
- Degree in Fashion Design or related field
- Strong sketching, Adobe Creative Suite, and technical design skills
- Understanding of garment construction and fabric properties
- Portfolio demonstrating original design work

DRAFT 5 — Formal / Corporate
================================================
Job Title: Apparel Merchandiser

Position Summary: The Apparel Merchandiser is responsible for planning, developing,
and managing product assortments to meet sales, brand, and customer experience
objectives.

Essential Duties and Responsibilities:
- Develops seasonal merchandising plans and assortment strategy
- Monitors inventory levels and coordinates replenishment
- Analyzes sales performance and adjusts merchandising strategy accordingly
- Collaborates with design, production, and marketing teams
- Ensures visual merchandising standards are maintained across locations

Minimum Qualifications:
- Bachelor's degree in Fashion Merchandising, Business, or related field
- Prior experience in apparel or retail merchandising
- Strong analytical and communication skills
