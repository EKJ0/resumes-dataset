JOB DESCRIPTIONS — ARCHITECT
Based on a sample of 14 CVs drawn from 144 resumes in the Architect category.

================================================
DRAFT 1 — Standard / Mid-Level
================================================
Job Title: Architect

Overview: We're seeking a licensed Architect to lead design development on
residential and commercial projects from concept through construction documents.

Key Responsibilities:
- Develop architectural designs and construction documents
- Coordinate with engineers, contractors, and clients throughout project lifecycle
- Ensure designs meet building codes, budget, and client requirements
- Prepare and present design proposals and drawings
- Oversee project timelines and manage design revisions

Requirements:
- Professional degree in Architecture; licensure preferred
- 3-6 years of architectural design experience
- Proficiency in Revit, AutoCAD, and Adobe Creative Suite
- Strong understanding of building codes and construction methods

================================================
DRAFT 2 — Senior / Project Lead
================================================
Job Title: Senior Architect / Project Architect

Overview: We're looking for a Senior Architect to lead project teams, manage client
relationships, and ensure design excellence across commercial and residential work.

Key Responsibilities:
- Lead architectural design from schematic design through construction administration
- Manage client relationships and present design solutions
- Oversee junior architects and coordinate with consultants and contractors
- Ensure projects meet budget, schedule, and quality standards
- Drive innovative, sustainable (LEED) design solutions

Requirements:
- Licensed Architect with 7+ years of experience
- Proven track record leading projects from concept to completion
- Advanced proficiency in Revit and BIM workflows
- LEED accreditation a plus

DRAFT 5 — Formal / Corporate
================================================
Job Title: Architect

Position Summary: The Architect is responsible for the design and technical
development of building projects, ensuring compliance with client requirements,
building codes, and industry standards.

Essential Duties and Responsibilities:
- Develops architectural designs from concept through construction documentation
- Coordinates with engineering consultants, contractors, and regulatory agencies
- Prepares and reviews drawings, specifications, and project budgets
- Presents design solutions to clients and internal stakeholders
- Ensures compliance with applicable building codes and standards

Minimum Qualifications:
- Professional degree in Architecture (B.Arch or M.Arch)
- State licensure or active pursuit of licensure preferred
- Minimum three (3) years of relevant design experience
- Proficiency in Revit and other industry-standard design software
