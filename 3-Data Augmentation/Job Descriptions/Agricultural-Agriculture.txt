================================================
DRAFT 1 — Standard / Farm Operations
================================================
Job Title: Agricultural Operations Specialist

Overview: We're seeking an Agricultural Operations professional to manage farm
equipment, crop operations, and day-to-day site maintenance.

Key Responsibilities:
- Operate and maintain agricultural equipment and machinery
- Perform routine maintenance, repairs, and safety inspections
- Support crop production, harvest, and inventory management
- Maintain accurate operational and maintenance records
- Ensure compliance with agricultural safety standards

Requirements:
- Experience in farm operations, equipment maintenance, or agriculture
- Working knowledge of agricultural machinery and repair
- Ability to perform physical labor outdoors in varying conditions
- Strong safety awareness and recordkeeping habits

DRAFT 2 — Farm Manager
================================================
Job Title: Farm Manager

Overview: We're looking for an experienced Farm Manager to oversee crop operations,
staff, and equipment across our agricultural operation.

Key Responsibilities:
- Plan and manage seasonal crop and land-use operations
- Supervise farm staff and coordinate seasonal labor needs
- Oversee equipment maintenance schedules and repairs
- Manage inventory of materials, supplies, and equipment
- Ensure compliance with agricultural and safety regulations

Requirements:
- 3+ years farm management or agricultural operations experience
- Relevant certification (e.g., pesticide application) a plus
- Strong leadership and organizational skills
- Familiarity with crop planning and yield optimization

DRAFT 5 — Formal / Corporate
================================================
Job Title: Agricultural Operations Specialist

Position Summary: The Agricultural Operations Specialist is responsible for the safe
and efficient operation, maintenance, and repair of agricultural equipment and
support of crop production activities.

Essential Duties and Responsibilities:
- Operates and maintains farm equipment in accordance with manufacturer specifications
- Performs scheduled and unscheduled equipment repairs
- Supports crop management operations including planting, cultivation, and harvest
- Maintains detailed maintenance and inventory records
- Adheres to all applicable safety and environmental regulations

Minimum Qualifications:
- High school diploma or equivalent; relevant certification preferred
- Prior experience in agricultural operations or equipment maintenance
- Ability to perform physical tasks in outdoor environments
