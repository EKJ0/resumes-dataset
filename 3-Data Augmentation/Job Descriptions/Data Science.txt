JOB DESCRIPTIONS — DATA SCIENCE
Based on a sample of 14 CVs drawn from 302 resumes in the Data Science category.

================================================
DRAFT 1 — Standard / Data Scientist
================================================
Job Title: Data Scientist

Overview: We're seeking a Data Scientist to build models, analyze data, and deliver
insights that inform business decisions.

Key Responsibilities:
- Analyze large datasets to identify trends and actionable insights
- Build and validate predictive models and machine learning pipelines
- Develop dashboards and reports using tools like Tableau
- Collaborate with engineering and business teams to deploy models
- Communicate findings clearly to technical and non-technical stakeholders

Requirements:
- Bachelor's/Master's degree in Data Science, Computer Science, or related field
- Proficiency in Python, SQL, and data visualization tools
- Experience with machine learning frameworks and statistical modeling
- Strong analytical and communication skills

================================================
DRAFT 2 — Senior / ML Engineer Angle
================================================
Job Title: Senior Data Scientist

Overview: We're looking for a Senior Data Scientist to lead advanced analytics
projects and mentor a growing data team.

Key Responsibilities:
- Lead end-to-end model development from problem framing to deployment
- Design and build data pipelines for large-scale analytics (AWS, SQL)
- Mentor junior data scientists and analysts
- Partner with stakeholders to define KPIs and measurement strategy
- Drive adoption of data science solutions across the business

Requirements:
- 5+ years data science/ML experience
- Advanced Python, SQL, and cloud (AWS) proficiency
- Strong experience with A/B testing and statistical inference
- Proven ability to translate complex analysis into business impact

DRAFT 3 — Concise Version

Job Title: Data Scientist

We have more data than answers — we need someone who can find the signal and turn
it into decisions people actually act on.

You'll:
- Explore data and build models that hold up in production
- Turn analysis into clear, actionable recommendations
- Build dashboards stakeholders will actually use
- Partner closely with engineering to ship what you build

You bring:
- Strong Python/SQL skills
- Real modeling and analytics experience
- The ability to explain "why" as clearly as "what"

DRAFT 4 — Skills/Tools-Focused
================================================
Job Title: Data Scientist

Core Skills Required: Python, SQL, machine learning, statistical modeling, Tableau/
Power BI, data pipelines, AWS/cloud infrastructure, A/B testing.

Responsibilities:
- Build and maintain machine learning models and pipelines
- Query and analyze data across multiple sources (SQL, cloud warehouses)
- Create visualizations and reports for stakeholder consumption
- Design and evaluate A/B tests and experiments
- Document methodology and maintain reproducible analysis

Qualifications:
- Degree in a quantitative field (CS, Statistics, Data Science)
- Hands-on experience with Python, SQL, and ML libraries
- Familiarity with cloud data infrastructure (AWS preferred)

================================================
DRAFT 5 — Formal / Corporate
================================================
Job Title: Data Scientist

Position Summary: The Data Scientist is responsible for applying statistical and
machine learning techniques to organizational data in order to generate actionable
business insights.

Essential Duties and Responsibilities:
- Designs and implements predictive models and analytical solutions
- Extracts, cleans, and analyzes data from multiple systems
- Develops visualizations and reports to communicate findings
- Collaborates with engineering and business teams on implementation
- Evaluates model performance and iterates based on results

Minimum Qualifications:
- Bachelor's degree in Data Science, Statistics, Computer Science, or related field
- Proficiency in Python, SQL, and statistical/ML methods
- Strong analytical, written, and verbal communication skills
