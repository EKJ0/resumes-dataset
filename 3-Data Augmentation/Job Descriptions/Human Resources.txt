DRAFT 1 — Standard / HR Generalist
================================================
Job Title: HR Generalist

Overview: We're seeking an HR Generalist to support employee relations, benefits
administration, and HR compliance across the organization.

Key Responsibilities:
- Administer employee benefits, compensation, and payroll support
- Support recruitment, onboarding, and offboarding processes
- Maintain accurate employee records and HR documentation
- Ensure compliance with employment laws and company policies
- Support performance management and training programs

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Human Resources or related field
- 2-4 years HR generalist experience
- Knowledge of employment law and HR compliance
- Strong organizational and interpersonal skills
